# Personal Research Website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web, including desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Purpose
赵斐然的个人科研主页。首次访问者应能理解研究身份、研究方向、项目关系及证据边界，并访问公开资料。

## Stack
Existing Astro static generation, plain CSS, existing GSAP. Cloudflare Pages is the established host.

## Confirmed constraints
- Research names, metrics, evidence boundaries, CV and identity stay grounded in supplied material.
- Research pages retain their readable warm white and deep blue academic language.
- Core links and content remain available on mobile and through the keyboard.
- Creative changes are previewed locally first; deployment follows an explicit user request.

## Personal space
The user requested a distinct space for freer personal work and life records, reached from About. The confirmed concept is vivid color and floating cards moving right to left. The latest approved layout is a shallow gallery arc with a larger central card and smaller, clearly separated side cards; mobile shows one full card with side peeks. Dragging changes speed and direction; clicking opens a card. The first version is a framework with explicit empty content slots, without photographs or invented personal records.

The confirmed opening uses a child's broad felt-tip drawing: a tiny boy in a vast grassland is approached in a slow opening shot, with a brief close-up of the flame and star in his hands. A rocket grows directly from the fire with the boy already in its window, then flies through forty named knowledge planets, gathers their light and enters the gallery through a meteor. There is no return shot or boarding action. It runs for about 23 seconds on the first eligible browser visit, with pause, skip and replay controls. Reduced motion bypasses automatic playback and offers a still scene on replay. The implementation is complete in local preview.
