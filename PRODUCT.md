## Personal space
The user requested a distinct space for freer personal work and life records, reached from About. The confirmed concept is vivid color and floating cards moving right to left. The latest approved layout is a shallow gallery arc with a larger central card and smaller, clearly separated side cards; mobile shows one full card with side peeks. Dragging changes speed and direction; clicking opens a card. The first version is a framework with explicit empty content slots, without photographs or invented personal records.

The confirmed opening uses a child's broad felt-tip drawing: a tiny boy in a vast grassland is approached in a slow opening shot, with a brief close-up of the flame and star in his hands. A rocket grows directly from the fire with the boy already in its window, then flies through forty named knowledge planets, gathers their light and enters the gallery through a meteor. There is no return shot or boarding action. It runs for about 23 seconds on the first eligible entry per tab session, with pause, skip and replay controls. Reloads and return navigation in that session skip the opening; a fresh tab session plays again, ignoring the former persistent record. Reduced motion bypasses automatic playback and offers a still scene on replay. The revised playback rule is ready in local preview.
